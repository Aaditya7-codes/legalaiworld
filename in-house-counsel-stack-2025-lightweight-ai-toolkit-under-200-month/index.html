Weekly Workflows You’ll Use

  • Draft NDAs & MSAs → Loio templates + Copilot/ChatGPT

  • Redline vendor paper → Loio clause scan + fallback playbook

  • Record & summarize exec calls → Otter.ai

  • Route signatures → Dropbox Sign

  • Maintain cookie banners & policies → Termly

  • Quick regulatory checks → Perplexity or Callidus AI

Setup in 60 Minutes

  1. Pick one stack above.

  2. Install Loio add-in for Word.

  3. Connect Dropbox Sign with templates.

  4. Turn on Otter.ai calendar auto-join.

  5. Publish cookie banner & privacy policy via Termly.

  6. Start using Callidus/ChatGPT/Copilot for contract drafting and quick memos.

Guardrails

  • Always verify: Use AI for speed, not as final counsel.

  • Check citations: Especially if drafting research memos.

  • Data hygiene: Don’t paste privileged info into unsecured AI tools.

  • One human in the loop: Final review stays with counsel.
